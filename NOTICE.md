# Notices

## The Shooting Star (Demo)

The original mod, *The Shooting Star (Demo)* by **rimuru.dev**
([CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-shooting-star-demo),
[TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@rimuru.dev)), is © rimuru.dev, All Rights Reserved.

None of its files are in this repository. The installer downloads the
original's jars from CurseForge to the player's own computer (exactly the files
listed by SHA-256 in `install/install.sh`) and builds the content mod there
from the files `install/files.tsv` names: its sounds, models, block and item
textures, fx shaders and space textures. `tools/films` runs the original's own
camera-path classes on that computer to record the cutscenes' uniforms.
